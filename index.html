Hangi verileri kullanır?

  • Hareket sensörleri: Yürüyüş adımlarını iPhone'un ve Apple Watch'un adım sayarıyla sayar.
  • Tercihlerin: Adın, hedeflerin, laf sokma seviyen, dil ve hatırlatıcı saatin yalnızca cihazında saklanır ve iPhone ile Apple Watch arasında doğrudan eşitlenir.
  • Bildirimler: Hatırlatıcılar cihazında planlanır; hiçbir sunucu kullanılmaz.

İsteğe bağlı AI koç: Yazıp gönderdiğin bahane, seçtiğin dil, üslup ve küfür tercihi Cloudflare Workers AI'da işlenir. Model Gemma'dır; istek Google'a ayrı bir API çağrısı olarak gönderilmez. Sağlık kayıtların, adın, kilon veya adım sayın bu isteğe eklenmez. Yazdığın metnin içinde kişisel bilgi varsa bu bilgi de gönderilir; kişisel ayrıntı yazmamanı öneririz. AI yanıtları hata yapabilir. İzni Koç ekranından veya profilinden kapatabilirsin; bu yeni istekleri durdurur, işlenmiş bir isteği geri alamaz. iPhone ve Watch izinleri ayrıdır.

English

NAG is provided by Arda Kaan Özcan. It does not require an account or display ads. Microsoft Clarity usage analytics on iPhone requires separate permission and is off by default. Health records stay on your devices. The optional AI coach uses Cloudflare only after you give permission on that device.

What it uses

  • Apple Health: reads your steps, weight check-ins, and walking workouts; writes the weight check-ins and walks you record in the app. On Apple Watch it reads heart rate during walks and saves the walk to Fitness. You can change these permissions in Apple Health at any time.
  • Motion sensors: counts the steps of your walks with the iPhone and Apple Watch pedometer.
  • Your preferences: name, goals, roast level, language, and reminder time are stored only on your devices and synced directly between iPhone and Apple Watch.
  • Notifications: reminders are scheduled on your device; no server is involved.

Optional AI coach: The excuse you submit, selected language, tone and profanity preference are processed by Cloudflare Workers AI using Gemma. No separate Google API request is made. Health records, name, weight and step counts are not attached to this request. Personal information you put in your text will also be transmitted; avoid including personal details. AI replies can be wrong. Disable permission in Coach or your profile to stop future requests; this cannot recall an already processed request. iPhone and Watch permissions are independent.

Server records: To verify the app and enforce limits, we retain the Apple App Attest key identifier, public key, app identifier, signature counter, last activity time and daily request counts. We do not save excuse or reply text to our server database or application logs. Daily counters expire at the end of the UTC day; device records are removed by the daily cleanup after 90 days of inactivity. Cloudflare processes connection metadata such as IP addresses to provide its service. Apple provides device attestation.

Optional usage analytics (Microsoft Clarity): If you opt in under Profile → Optional analytics, the iPhone app starts Microsoft Clarity. Microsoft may process taps, scrolling, navigation, session duration, reconstructions of the masked interface (session replays), SDK-generated pseudonymous session/user identifiers, device/operating system information and performance data. Approximate location may be derived from IP; GPS location is not requested. We use this to find usability problems and improve NAG. NAG does not send custom user identities, names, health values or coach text. Screen contents and text inputs are marked for masking on the device. We do not attach health data to Clarity tags or events. The SDK does not run on Apple Watch or in widgets.

Managing analytics consent: Clarity is not initialized before opt-in; other app features remain available. Turning the same profile switch off pauses new session capture and informs the SDK that consent has been withdrawn; it does not automatically delete data already sent. NAG does not configure analytics for advertising targeting or cross-app tracking. Microsoft processes data under the Microsoft Privacy Statement. Under Clarity’s published retention policy, session replay data is retained for 30 days; labeled/favorited sessions and aggregated interaction/heatmap data may be retained for 9 months. Microsoft’s service policies may change; see the linked policy for current periods.

Legal basis and transfers: We rely on consent for optional analytics, performance of the requested service for AI replies, and our legitimate interest in providing a secure service for device verification and abuse prevention. Microsoft and Cloudflare may process information on infrastructure outside your country. Depending on applicable law, you can contact us below to exercise rights of access, correction, deletion, objection, portability and withdrawal of consent; you can also complain to your relevant data protection authority. You do not need to include health records in a request.

Sharing: Health data is never used for advertising, marketing, or data sales, and is not shared with third parties. NAG does not store Health data in iCloud.

Deletion: You can send analytics deletion requests to our contact address. Because we do not attach direct identity details, additional information may be needed to locate the relevant session. Deleting the app removes your preferences; server verification records expire according to the retention period above. Records written to Apple Health can be deleted in the Health app.
